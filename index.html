Nova Scotia Reality

4.5

per 100,000 — Highest or near-highest police-reported human trafficking rate in Canada (2024). National average: 1.5. Decade average: 4.1.

48

incidents reported in 2024 alone. Coastal ports and corridors make NS a key node for movement of victims.

93%

of victims are women and girls; two-thirds under 25. Most accused are men and boys.

$150k

provincial reward for information on Lilly & Jack Sullivan (missing since May 2, 2025, Lansdowne Station).

Higher reporting reflects better awareness and specialized units, not just higher incidence. Labour trafficking is an emerging under-reported issue affecting newcomers.
